### Signal
For people that know me personally, contact me using [Signal](https://signal.org/). If you have my phone number, you can already contact me in the app.

If you don't have my phone number, ask me for my Signal username or phone number using another method (i.e SimpleX, email or in person).

I don't share my phone number and Signal username publicly, because I want to keep them limited to people who know me in real life.
